AI output is an estimate. In particular:

  • it can misidentify foods, misjudge portion sizes, or miss ingredients that aren’t visible, such as oils, sauces, sugar or fillings;
  • calorie and macronutrient values can be inaccurate or incomplete;
  • it may decline to analyze a photo, report that no food was found, or fail. You can try again, but we do not guarantee that any photo will be analyzed;
  • we check AI-generated targets against limits based on standard equations and may replace them with a formula-based estimate, so your targets may not be exactly what the AI suggested.

Use AI output as a helpful guide, not as exact or authoritative information, and rely on your own judgment.
